### 3.1 \(\colorbox{#dcfce7}{\color{#087443}{\text{正确}}}\)

**原题**

设\(f(x)\)满足\(f(0)=0\)，且\(f'(0)\)存在，则
\(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f(1-\cos x)}{\ln(1-x\sin x)}=\underline{\qquad}.\)

**我的解答**

\(f(0)=0\qquad f'(0)\)存在，求\(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f(1-\cos x)}{\ln(1-x\sin x)}\)

令\(u=1-\cos x\)

\(\Rightarrow\lim_{u\to0}\frac{f(u)-f(0)}{u-0}
=\frac{f(1-\cos x)}{1-\cos x}=f'(0)\)
\(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f(1-\cos x)}{\ln(1-x\sin x)}
=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f(1-\cos x)}{1-\cos x}\cdot
\frac{1-\cos x}{\ln(1-x\sin x)}
=-\frac12\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f(1-\cos x)}{1-\cos x}\)
\(\Rightarrow\lim=-\frac12f'(0)\)

### 3.2 \(\colorbox{#dcfce7}{\color{#087443}{\text{正确}}}\)

**原题**

设\(F(x)=g(x)\varphi(x)\)，\(\varphi(x)\)在\(x=a\)处连续但不可导，\(g(x)\)在\(x=a\)处可导，\(F(x)\)在\(x=a\)处可导，则一定有（~~~）。

(A) \(g(a)=0\)\quad(B) \(g(a)\ne0\)\quad(C) \(g'(a)=0\)\quad(D) \(g(a)\)可以为任意实数

**我的解答**

\(F(x)=g(x)\varphi(x)\qquad \varphi(x)\)在\(x=a\)处连续而不可导

\(g(x)\)在\(x=a\)处连续可导\qquad 求\(F(x)\)在\(x=a\)处可导

\(\Rightarrow\lim_{x\to a}\frac{F(x)-F(a)}{x-a}
=\frac{g(x)\varphi(x)-g(a)\varphi(a)}{x-a}
\overset{\text{存在}}{=}A\)
\(\Rightarrow x\to a\text{时， }g(x)\varphi(x)-g(a)\varphi(a)\to0\)
\(g'(a)=\lim_{x\to a}\frac{g(x)-g(a)}{x-a}\overset{\text{存在}}{=}B\)
\(\Rightarrow x\to a\text{时， }g(x)-g(a)\to0\)
\(\sout{\text{设 }\varphi(a)=c}\quad
g(a)\varphi(x)-g(a)\varphi(a)=g(a)(\varphi(x)-\varphi(a))\to0\)
\(\sout{\Rightarrow x\to a\text{时， }g(x)\varphi(a)-g(a)\varphi(a)
=\varphi(a)(g(x)-g(a))\to0}\)
然而\(\varphi(x)\)在\(x=a\)不可导
\(\Rightarrow\lim_{x\to a}\frac{\varphi(x)-\varphi(a)}{x-a}
\overset{\text{不存在}}{=}C\)
\(\Rightarrow\lim_{x\to a}\text{时， }\varphi(x)-\varphi(a)\overset{x}{\to}0\)
\(\Rightarrow g(a)\text{必为}0\)
\(\Rightarrow A\)

### 3.3 \(\colorbox{#dcfce7}{\color{#087443}{\text{正确}}}\)

**原题**

设连续函数\(f(x)\)满足
\(\lim_{x\to2}\frac{\ln(x-1)}{f(3-x)}=2,\qquad f(1)=0,\)
则\(f'(1)\)的值为（~~~）。

(A) \(2\)\quad(B) \(-2\)\quad(C) \(\frac12\)\quad(D) \(-\frac12\)

**我的解答**

\(\lim_{x\to2}\frac{\ln(x-1)}{f(3-x)}=2\qquad f(1)=0\qquad \text{求 }f'(1)\)
\(f'(1)=\lim_{x\to1}\frac{f(x)-f(1)}{x-1}
=\frac{f(x)}{x-1}\lim_{x\to1}\)
令\(u=3-x\to1\)，则\(x\to2\)
\(\lim_{x\to2}\frac{\ln(x-1)}{2-x}\cdot\frac{2-x}{f(3-x)}
=\lim_{x\to2}\frac{\ln(x-1)}{2-x}\cdot f'(1)^{-1}
=\lim_{x\to2}-\frac1{f'(1)}=2\)
\(\Rightarrow f'(1)=-\frac12\Rightarrow D\)

### 3.4 \(\colorbox{#dcfce7}{\color{#087443}{\text{正确}}}\)

**原题**

设\(f(x)\)在\(x=0\)处可导，\(f(0)=f'(0)=\sqrt2\)，则
\(\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f^2(x)-2}{x}=\underline{\qquad}.\)

**我的解答**

\(f(x)\)在\(x=0\)处可导\qquad \(f(0)=f'(0)=\sqrt2\)\qquad 求\(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f^2(x)-2}{x}\)

\(f'(0)=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f(x)-f(0)}{x-0}
=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f^2(x)-f^2(0)}{x(f(x)+f(0))}
=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f^2(x)-2}{x(f(x)+\sqrt2)}=\sqrt2\)
\(\Rightarrow\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f^2(x)-2}{x}
=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f^2(x)-2}{x(f(x)+\sqrt2)}
\cdot\frac{f(x)+\sqrt2}{1}
=\lim_{x\to0}\sqrt2(\sqrt2+f(x))=4\)

### 3.5 \(\colorbox{#dcfce7}{\color{#087443}{\text{正确}}}\)

=\lim_{x\to0^+}\frac{\frac1{n^2}}x

\(\Rightarrow\text{递减有下界， }\lim_{x\to0^+}\frac1{xn^2}=f'_+(0)=1\)

### 3.6 \(\colorbox{#dcfce7}{\color{#087443}{\text{正确}}}\)

**原题**

设可导函数\(f(x)>0\)，则
\(\lim_{n\to\infty}n\ln\frac{f(1/n)}{f(0)}=\underline{\qquad}.\)

**我的解答**

可导函数\ \sout{\(f(x)\)}\ \(f(x)>0\)\qquad 求\(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}n\ln\frac{f(\frac1n)}{f(0)}\)
\(\lim_{n\to\infty}n\ln\frac{f(\frac1n)}{f(0)}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\ln\frac{f(\frac1n)}{f(0)}}{\frac1n}\)
\(=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\ln\left(1+\frac{f(\frac1n)}{f(0)}-1\right)}{\frac1n}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f(\frac1n)-f(0)}{\frac1n\cdot f(0)}\)
\(f'(0)=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f(x)-f(0)}{x-0}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f(\frac1n)-f(0)}{\frac1n}\)
\(\Rightarrow\lim_{n\to\infty}
\frac{f(\frac1n)-f(0)}{\frac1n}\cdot
\frac{\frac1n}{\frac1n\cdot f(0)}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}f'(0)\cdot\frac1{f(0)}
=\frac{f'(0)}{f(0)}\)

### 3.8 \(\colorbox{#dcfce7}{\color{#087443}{\text{正确}}}\)

**原题**

设函数\(f(x)\)连续，
\(\lim_{x\to1}\frac{f(x)-1}{\ln x}=2,\)
则曲线\(y=f(x)\)在\(x=1\)处的切线方程为\(\underline{\qquad}\)。

**我的解答**

\(f(x)\)连续\qquad \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to1}\frac{f(x)-1}{\ln x}=2\)\qquad 求\(y=f(x)\)在\(x=1\)处切线方程
\(\lim_{x\to1}\frac{f(x)-1}{\ln x}
=\lim_{x\to1}\frac{f(x)-1}{\ln(1+x-1)}
=\lim_{x\to1}\frac{f(x)-1}{x-1}
=\lim_{x\to1}\frac{f(x)-1}{x-1}=2\)
\(\Rightarrow f(1)=2x-1=1\)
\(\Rightarrow f'(1)=\lim_{x\to1}\frac{f(x)-f(1)}{x-1}
=\frac{f(x)-1}{\ln x}=2\)
\(\Rightarrow\text{切线方程 }\sout{y=2x}\quad
y-1=f'(1)(x-1)=2(x-1)\Rightarrow y=2x-1\)

### 3.11 \(\colorbox{#fee2e2}{\color{#b42318}{\text{需修正}}}\)

**原题**

设\(\varphi(x)\)具有一阶连续导数，
\(f(x)=\varphi(x)[1+|\ln(1+x)|],\)
则\(\varphi(0)=0\)是\(f(x)\)在\(x=0\)处可导的（~~~）。

(A) 充分必要条件\quad(B) 充分非必要条件\quad(C) 必要非充分条件\quad(D) 既非充分又非必要条件

**我的解答**

\(\varphi\)具一阶连续导数\qquad
\(f(x)=\varphi(x)[1+|\ln(1+x)|]\)\qquad
求\(f(x)\)在\(x=0\)可导\(\Leftrightarrow?\Rightarrow\varphi(0)=0\)

由\ \sout{\(\varphi(0)=0\)}\ \(f(x)\)在\(x=0\)可导：
\(f'_-(0)=f'_+(0)\Rightarrow\varphi(x)\ln(1+x)=0,\ x\to0
\Rightarrow\varphi(0)\ln1=0\)
\color{#b42318}{\(\ln1\)本身等于\(0\)，对\(\varphi(0)\)任意常数即可\(\Rightarrow\)不必要条件}

由\(\varphi(0)=0\)：
\(\Rightarrow f(0)=0\)
\(\Rightarrow f'_-(0)=\lim_{x\to0^-}\frac{\varphi(x)[1-\ln(1+x)]}{x}
=\lim_{x\to0^-}\frac{\varphi(x)-x\varphi(x)}x
=\lim_{x\to0^-}\frac{\varphi(x)}x-\varphi(0)
=\lim_{x\to0^-}\frac{\varphi(x)}x\)
\(f'_+(0)=\lim_{x\to0^+}\frac{\varphi(x)+x\varphi(x)}x
=\lim_{x\to0^+}\frac{\varphi(x)}x=f'_-(0)\)
\(\Rightarrow\text{充分条件}\)

**正确解答**

保留原解答中“\(\varphi(0)=0\)时可导”的部分。

\color{#087443}{将“不必要条件”改为“必要条件”，最终选A。}
\(f'_+(0)=\varphi'(0)+\varphi(0),\qquad
f'_-(0)=\varphi'(0)-\varphi(0).\)
\(f'_+(0)=f'_-(0)\Longleftrightarrow\varphi(0)=0.\)
原解答在标注处只代入了函数值；判断可导必须比较左右导数。

### 3.12 \(\colorbox{#dcfce7}{\color{#087443}{\text{正确}}}\)

**原题**

设\(f(x)\)在\(x_0\)处可导，\(x_n=\sin\frac1n+\frac1{n^2}\)，则
\(\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f(x_0+\frac1n)-f(x_0-x_n)}{\sin\frac1n}
=\underline{\qquad}.\)

**我的解答**

\(f(x)\)在\(x_0\)处可导\qquad \(x_n=\sin\frac1n+\frac1{n^2}\)\qquad
求\(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f(x_0+\frac1n)-f(x_0-x_n)}{\sin\frac1n}\)

\sout{令\(t=\frac1n\)}

\sout{\(\displaystyle\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(x_0+t)-f(x_0-x_n)}{\sin t}\)}

\sout{\(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f(x_0+\frac1n)-f(x_0-x_n)}{\sin\frac1n}\)}

\sout{（中间洛必达计算）}

当\(n\to\infty\)时，有\(\frac1n\to0\)，亦有\(x_n\to0\)
\(\Rightarrow f'(x_0)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f(x_0+\frac1n)-f(x_0)}{\frac1n}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f(x_0-x_n)-f(x_0)}{-x_n}\)
\(\Rightarrow\lim_{n\to\infty}f(x_0+\tfrac1n)=f'(x_0)\cdot\tfrac1n+f(x_0)\)
\(\lim_{n\to\infty}f(x_0-x_n)=f(x_0)-f'(x_0)\cdot x_n\)
\(\Rightarrow\lim_{n\to\infty}
\frac{f(x_0+\frac1n)-f(x_0-x_n)}{\sin\frac1n}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f'(x_0)\cdot\frac1n+f'(x_0)\cdot x_n}{\frac1n}\)
\(=\lim_{n\to\infty}f'(x_0)\left(1+\frac{x_n}{\frac1n}\right)
=\lim_{n\to\infty}f'(x_0)\left(1+1+\frac1n\right)
=2f'(x_0)\)

### 3.15 \(\colorbox{#fee2e2}{\color{#b42318}{\text{需修正}}}\)

**原题**

设
\(f(x)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{x^2e^{n(x-1)}+ax+b}{5+e^{n(x-1)}},\)
求\(f(x)\)并讨论\(f(x)\)的连续性及可导性与\(a,b\)的关系。

**我的解答**

\(f(x)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{x^2e^{n(x-1)}+ax+b}{5+e^{n(x-1)}}
\qquad\text{求 }f(x)\qquad
\text{讨论 }f(x)\text{连续性}\)

\(\text{可导性}\text{与 }a,b\text{的关系}\)
当\(x>1\)时，
\(\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{x^2e^{n(x-1)}+ax+b}{5+e^{n(x-1)}}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{x^2e^{n(x-1)}\cdot n}{e^{n(x-1)}\cdot n}=x^2\)
当\(x=1\)时，
\(\lim_{n\to\infty}=\frac{x^2+ax+b}{5+1}=\frac{1+a+b}{6}\)
当\(0<x<1\)时，
\(\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{x^2e^{n(x-1)}+ax+b}{5+e^{n(x-1)}}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{ax+b}{5}=\frac{ax+b}{5}\)
当\(x=0\)时，
\(\lim_{n\to\infty}=\frac{b}{5+e^{-n}}=\frac b5\)
当\(x<0\)时，
\(\lim_{n\to\infty}=\frac{ax+b}{5}\)
\(\Rightarrow f(x)=

\dfrac{ax+b}{5},\quad x<1,\)

\([3pt]
\dfrac{1+a+b}{6},\quad x=1,\)

\([3pt]
x^2,\quad x>1.\)
若\(f(x)\)连续，则
\(\lim_{x\to1^-}f(x)=\lim_{x\to1^+}f(x)=f(1)
\Rightarrow a+b=5\text{时，}f(x)\text{连续}\)
若\(f(x)\)可导，则
\(f'_-(1)=f'_+(1)\Rightarrow
\color{#b42318}{a+b=5\text{时，}f(x)\text{可导}}\)

**正确解答**

保留原解答求出的分段函数和连续条件\(a+b=5\)。

\color{#087443}{将最后一行改为：}
\(f'_-(1)=\frac a5,\qquad f'_+(1)=2.\)
\(\frac a5=2\Rightarrow a=10,\qquad a+b=5\Rightarrow b=-5.\)
\(\boxed{f\text{连续}\Longleftrightarrow a+b=5},\qquad
\boxed{f\text{可导}\Longleftrightarrow a=10,\ b=-5}.\)
原解答在标注处把连续条件直接当成可导条件，漏掉了左右导数相等的计算。
